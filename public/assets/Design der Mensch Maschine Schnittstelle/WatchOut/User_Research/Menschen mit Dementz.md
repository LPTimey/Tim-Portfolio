## Symptoms

![bg right:33%](./Pictures_presentation/advanced-dementia-signs-behaviors-in-seniors.jpg)

* Memory loss
* Challenges in planning or solving problems
* Difficulty completing tasks
* Confusion with time or place
* Trouble understanding images and spatial imagination
* Problems with words (speaking or writing)
* Misplacing things and losing the ability to retrace steps
* Decreased judgment
* Withdrawal from work or social activities
* Changes in mood and personality

---

## Resulting Problems

* Difficulties in pursuing hobbies
  * worsens quality of life and mental health
* Health issues
  * less exercise and healthy diet
* Low confidence
  * makes a healthy lifestyle more difficult
